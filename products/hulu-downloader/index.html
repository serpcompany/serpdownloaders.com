Hulu Downloader

serp.ly/hulu-downloader

Cancel Hulu whenever you want — but keep the shows. Download episodes as MP4 files you own forever.

Overview

The moment you cancel Hulu, every show you ever watched disappears. Even while you are paying, content rotates off the platform without warning. On the ad-supported plan, commercials interrupt every few minutes. This extension saves Hulu shows, movies, and originals as clean MP4 files that live on your computer permanently.

Browse Hulu in your browser as normal, find what you want, and click download. The extension grabs the video at up to 1080p and saves it with subtitles embedded. On the ad-supported plan, it automatically detects commercial breaks and strips them out so your downloaded files play straight through. Need to save a whole season before a road trip? Queue it up and the extension downloads every episode in order, properly named by season and episode number.

All processing runs locally in your browser. Nothing gets uploaded anywhere, your watch history stays private, and the files work in any media player on any device. Try 3 downloads free before you buy.

Why It Exists

  • Watch Hulu content offline on flights, commutes, or devices without Hulu app support
  • Build a permanent personal library of your favorite series without expiration dates
  • Save bandwidth by downloading episodes once over Wi-Fi instead of re-streaming on mobile
  • Keep your Hulu viewing habits private with fully local processing and no cloud relay

Key Features

  • Download Hulu shows, movies, and originals in up to 1080p quality as MP4 files
  • Batch download entire seasons with automatic season and episode numbering
  • Automatic ad segment detection for cleaner downloads on ad-supported Hulu plans
  • Embed multiple subtitle tracks and audio streams directly in the output file
  • Support for Hulu Live TV recordings and DVR content where available
  • Resume interrupted downloads without re-fetching completed stream segments
  • Choose between available quality levels with file size estimates before downloading
  • Organize downloads by series name, network, or genre with custom folder naming
  • Duplicate detection prevents re-downloading episodes already in your library
  • Works on Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave, and Opera on Windows, macOS, and Linux

How It Works

  • Install and sign in: Add Hulu Downloader to your browser and log into your Hulu account as you normally would.
  • Browse Hulu content: Navigate to any show, movie, or Hulu Original and the extension detects the player and available quality options.
  • Download or queue episodes: Download individual titles or queue full seasons for batch processing with automatic episode naming and ad segment detection.
  • Watch offline ad-free: The finished MP4 files with embedded subtitles play on any device in any media player, without commercial interruptions.

Privacy and Permissions

Supported regions: Worldwide.

Limitations:

  • Active Hulu subscription and browser login required.
  • Maximum quality is limited to what Hulu delivers to the browser (up to 1080p).
  • Ad segment detection may not catch every ad break on all content.
  • Live TV must finish broadcasting before DVR recordings can be downloaded.
  • Download speeds should be kept reasonable to avoid Hulu's abuse detection.
  • Safari and mobile browsers are not supported.

Permissions:

  • downloads: Saves completed MP4 video files to your local downloads folder after stream processing.
  • activeTab: Detects Hulu's video player on the current page to display download options only on Hulu pages.
  • storage: Stores quality preferences, download queue state, and completed download history locally in the browser.
  • notifications: Sends a notification when episode downloads or batch season queues finish processing.

FAQ

Does it work with both ad-supported and no-ads Hulu plans?

Yes. On ad-supported plans, the downloader detects commercial segments and can skip them during the download process. No-ads subscribers get clean downloads directly. Both plan types are fully supported.

Can I download entire seasons of Hulu series at once?

Yes. Navigate to a series page, queue individual episodes or full seasons, and the extension processes them sequentially. Each file is named with the series title, season number, and episode number for easy organization.

Does it support Hulu Live TV recordings?

DVR recordings from Hulu + Live TV can be downloaded if they play in your browser. Live broadcasts must finish before they can be captured. The extension uses your existing Hulu session and subscription access level.

What about subtitles and closed captions?

Available subtitle and closed caption tracks are embedded directly into the MP4 file. You can toggle them on or off in any compatible media player like VLC, MPV, or Plex.

Will Hulu know I'm downloading?

The extension accesses streams through your normal browser session, the same way the Hulu player does. We recommend using reasonable download speeds and a VPN for additional privacy. The extension never shares session data or credentials externally.
