Nicovideo Downloader

Download Niconico videos complete with the legendary scrolling comments. Full quality, zero buffering, fully offline.

Overview

Half the magic of Niconico is watching comments race across the screen, but the platform's sluggish servers and Premium-exclusive quality tiers can turn the experience into a frustrating ordeal. Nicovideo Downloader captures your favorite videos as polished MP4 files and exports the scrolling comments as subtitle files you can load into VLC or MPV. All the magic, none of the buffering.

Whether you are preserving Vocaloid classics, rescuing MAD videos before they get pulled, or building a library of Japanese learning material, this tool does it all. Batch download entire mylist playlists, keep the original Japanese titles and video IDs in your filenames, and get Premium-tier quality when your account supports it.

Everything runs locally in your browser. Your Niconico activity stays completely private.

How It Works

  • Install and log in: Add Nicovideo Downloader to your browser and sign into your Niconico account for access to your authorized content.

  • Download with comments: Choose your quality tier, optionally enable danmaku comment export, and start the download or queue multiple videos.
  • Watch with scrolling comments: Load the MP4 and ASS subtitle file together in VLC or MPV to experience Niconico's signature scrolling comments offline.

FAQ

Does the comment overlay work offline?

Yes. Comments are exported as ASS subtitle files that faithfully reproduce the scrolling overlay effect. Load the ASS file alongside the MP4 in VLC or MPV and the comments will scroll across the video just like they do on the Niconico website.

Do I need a Niconico Premium account?

A free Niconico account gives you downloads at standard quality. Premium subscribers unlock higher bitrate streams and bypass economy-hour restrictions. The downloader uses your existing session, so your download quality mirrors your account tier.

Can I download from mylist playlists?

Yes. Head to any public or personal mylist page and the downloader identifies all videos in the list. Queue them for batch processing and each video downloads with its correct title and video ID.

Are Japanese file names preserved?

Yes. The downloader preserves original Japanese titles in filenames along with the Niconico video ID (sm/nm number). This makes it effortless to identify and organize your downloaded library.

What about Niconico Live (nama housou) recordings?

Live broadcasts that have been archived as timeshift recordings can be downloaded if they are still accessible for viewing. Active live streams cannot be captured during broadcasting.
