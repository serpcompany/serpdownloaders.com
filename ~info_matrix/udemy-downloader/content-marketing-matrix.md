#### FAQ

**Q: How do I download a video?**
**A:** Log in to Udemy, navigate to a lecture page in a course you're enrolled in, then click the download button on the player or the extension icon. The video will download as MP4.

**Q: Can I download an entire course at once?**
**A:** Yes. The extension lists all lectures in the course with checkboxes. Select the lectures you want and click "Download All" to queue them.

**Q: What quality options are available?**
**A:** The extension automatically selects the best available quality from Udemy's servers — up to 4K (2160p). Available resolutions depend on what Udemy provides for each lecture.

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All videos are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player. For DASH streams, audio and video are automatically merged using ffmpeg.wasm.

**Q: Do I need to be enrolled in the course?**
**A:** Yes. The extension uses your Udemy login session to access course videos. You must be enrolled (free or paid) in the course to download its lectures.

**Q: Why does it say "DRM protected"?**
**A:** Some Udemy courses use DRM (Digital Rights Management) to prevent downloads. These videos cannot be downloaded by any browser extension. The extension will notify you when this is the case.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited downloads.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Where are my downloads saved?**
**A:** Videos are saved via your browser's save-as dialog. You can configure a default download folder name in the extension settings.

**Q: Is my Udemy account safe?**
**A:** Yes. The extension reads your existing Udemy login cookies to authenticate API requests — it never stores or transmits your Udemy credentials. SERP activation uses secure OTP — no passwords stored.
