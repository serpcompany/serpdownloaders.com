#### FAQ

**Q: How do I record a live stream?**
**A:** Go to any live model page on Cams.com. Open the extension popup or click the in-page download button. Select your quality and click Start. The extension records the stream in real-time. Click Stop when you're done — the recording saves as MP4.

**Q: How do I download a recorded video?**
**A:** Navigate to a model's video page (/{model}/videos/{id}). The extension detects the VOD automatically. Select your quality and click Download.

**Q: What quality options are available?**
**A:** The extension parses the HLS master manifest and shows all available variants — typically multiple resolutions with frame rates (e.g., 1080p, 720p @ 60fps). Formats are sorted by quality (highest first).

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All videos are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player.

**Q: Where are my downloads saved?**
**A:** Videos automatically save to a `CAMSCOM` subfolder inside your browser's default Downloads directory.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited downloads.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Why does it say the model is offline?**
**A:** The model must be currently live for stream capture. For offline models, check their video page for recorded VOD content.

**Q: Why does it say 'private show'?**
**A:** The extension cannot capture private shows. Wait for the model to return to public broadcast.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. All stream processing happens entirely in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP — no passwords stored.
