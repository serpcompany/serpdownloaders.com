#### FAQ

**Q: How do I download a video?**
**A:** Go to any video page on Dailymotion.com. Click the extension icon in your toolbar, wait for the scan to complete, then select your preferred quality and click Download.

**Q: What quality options are available?**
**A:** The extension detects all qualities available for each video — typically 1080p, 720p, 480p, 360p, and 240p. The exact options depend on what the uploader provided. Formats are sorted by quality (highest first).

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All videos are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player.

**Q: Where are my downloads saved?**
**A:** Videos automatically save to a `Dailymotion` subfolder inside your browser's default Downloads directory.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited downloads.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Why isn't the extension finding my video?**
**A:** Make sure you are on a Dailymotion video page (the URL should contain `/video/`). Click Rescan in the popup after the page has fully loaded. If issues persist, refresh the page and try again.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. Video processing happens entirely in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP — no passwords stored. No data is sent to third-party servers.

**Q: Does this work with embedded Dailymotion videos?**
**A:** The extension works on Dailymotion.com video pages. For embedded videos on other sites, navigate to the original Dailymotion video page first.
