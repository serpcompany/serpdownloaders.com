#### FAQ

**Q: How do I capture a live stream?**
**A:** Go to any model page on XLoveCam.com while the model is live. Open the popup or click the download button on the player. Select quality and click Start. Click Stop when you want to end the recording.

**Q: Can I download recorded videos too?**
**A:** Yes. Navigate to a recorded video page on XLoveCam, and the extension will detect it as VOD content with a standard Download button instead of Start/Stop.

**Q: What quality options are available?**
**A:** The extension parses the HLS master manifest for all available resolutions — typically multiple quality levels. Formats are sorted by quality (highest first).

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All videos are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player.

**Q: Where are my downloads saved?**
**A:** Videos automatically save to an `XLOVECAM` subfolder inside your browser's default Downloads directory.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited downloads.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Does this support VR streams?**
**A:** Yes. The extension detects VR streams and includes proper camera settings metadata. A VR badge appears in the popup when VR content is detected.

**Q: Why isn't the extension finding the stream?**
**A:** Make sure the model is currently live and broadcasting. The extension needs an active stream to detect. If the model is offline or in a private show, the extension will show a status message.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. Video processing happens entirely in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP — no passwords stored.
