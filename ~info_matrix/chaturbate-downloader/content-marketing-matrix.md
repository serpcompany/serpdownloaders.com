#### FAQ

**Q: How do I record a live stream?**
**A:** Go to any model's page on Chaturbate.com while they're live. Click the download button on the player or the extension icon, select your quality, and click Start. The extension records the stream in real time. Click Stop when you're done — the recording saves as MP4.

**Q: Can I download saved videos (VOD)?**
**A:** Yes. Navigate to a model's video page (/{model}/videos/{id}). The extension detects the recorded video and lets you download it directly.

**Q: What quality options are available?**
**A:** The extension parses the HLS master manifest and shows all available quality variants — typically from 240p up to 1080p at 60fps. Higher resolutions appear first.

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All videos are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player.

**Q: Where are my downloads saved?**
**A:** Videos automatically save to a `CHATURBATE` subfolder inside your browser's default Downloads directory.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited downloads.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Why does the extension say the model is offline?**
**A:** The model must be currently broadcasting for live capture to work. If they're offline, the extension will show a status message. For saved videos, navigate to their videos page instead.

**Q: What about private shows?**
**A:** The extension only works with public streams. Private, hidden, or paid shows are not accessible.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. Stream processing happens entirely in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP — no passwords stored.
