#### FAQ

**Q: How do I download a video?**
**A:** Go to any video page on RedGifs.com. Then click the download button on the player, click the extension icon, or right-click and select "Download RedGifs Video."

**Q: What quality options are available?**
**A:** The extension retrieves all available qualities from the RedGifs API — typically multiple resolutions. Formats are sorted by quality (highest first), with MP4 preferred over HLS.

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All videos are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player.

**Q: Where are my downloads saved?**
**A:** Videos automatically save to a `RedGifs` subfolder inside your browser's default Downloads directory.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited downloads.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Can I download from feed pages?**
**A:** Yes. The extension scans feed and search pages, detecting multiple videos. Use the side panel to see all detected videos, images, and text.

**Q: Why isn't the extension finding my video?**
**A:** Make sure you are on a RedGifs.com page. If the video is not detected, try refreshing the page. The extension needs the page to fully load before detection works.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. Video processing happens entirely in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP — no passwords stored.
