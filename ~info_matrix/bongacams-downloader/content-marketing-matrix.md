#### FAQ

**Q: How do I record a live stream?**
**A:** Go to any model page on BongaCams.com while the stream is live. Click the download button on the player or the extension icon. Select your quality and click Start. The extension records in real time. Click Stop when you're done — the recording saves as MP4.

**Q: How do I download a VOD recording?**
**A:** Navigate to a model's Videos page on BongaCams and select a recording. Click the extension icon, choose your quality, and hit Download. The recording saves as MP4.

**Q: What quality options are available?**
**A:** The extension detects all available qualities from the HLS master manifest — typically multiple resolutions up to 1080p. Formats are sorted by quality (highest first).

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All videos are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player.

**Q: Where are my downloads saved?**
**A:** Videos automatically save to a `BONGACAMS` subfolder inside your browser's default Downloads directory.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial captures after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited captures.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Why isn't the extension finding the stream?**
**A:** Make sure the model is currently live. If the model is offline or in a private show, the extension will display a status message. Refresh the page if issues persist.

**Q: Does this support VR streams?**
**A:** Yes. The extension detects VR streams and preserves stereoscopic metadata (stereoPacking, frameFormat, horizontalAngle) in the filename.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. All stream processing happens entirely in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP — no passwords stored.
