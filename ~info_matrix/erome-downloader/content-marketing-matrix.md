#### FAQ

**Q: How do I download a video?**
**A:** Go to any album or video page on EroMe.com. Download buttons appear on each media item. Click the button on a video to see quality options and download, or right-click and select "Download Erome Video."

**Q: Can I download images from albums?**
**A:** Yes. The extension detects both videos and images in EroMe albums. Each image gets its own download button. Images are saved in their original format at full resolution.

**Q: What quality options are available?**
**A:** The extension detects all available qualities from the source. Formats are sorted by quality (highest first), with MP4 preferred over HLS.

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All videos are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player.

**Q: Where are my downloads saved?**
**A:** Videos and images automatically save to an `Erome` subfolder inside your browser's default Downloads directory.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited downloads.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Why isn't the extension finding my video?**
**A:** Make sure you are on an EroMe album or video page. The extension needs the page to fully load before detection works. If issues persist, refresh the page.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. Video and image processing happens entirely in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP — no passwords stored.
