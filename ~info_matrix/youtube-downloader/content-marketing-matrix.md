#### FAQ

**Q: How do I download a YouTube video?**
**A:** Go to any video on YouTube.com and press play. Click the download button that appears on the player, or click the extension icon in your toolbar. Select your preferred quality and click Download.

**Q: What quality options are available?**
**A:** The extension queries multiple YouTube API clients to find every available format — typically from 144p up to 4K, depending on the video. Progressive (combined audio+video) formats are preferred, with separate audio/video streams merged automatically when needed.

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All videos are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player. When separate video and audio streams are downloaded, they are automatically merged into a single MP4.

**Q: Where are my downloads saved?**
**A:** The browser's save-as dialog appears so you can choose where to save. There is no forced download folder.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited downloads.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Can I download YouTube videos embedded on other websites?**
**A:** Yes. The extension detects YouTube iframes and links on any website and shows download buttons next to them.

**Q: Why isn't the extension finding my video?**
**A:** Press play on the video first. The extension needs the video to load before it can extract formats. If issues persist, refresh the page and try again.

**Q: What is the yt-dlp command feature?**
**A:** For advanced users, the popup includes buttons to copy yt-dlp commands for Mac and Windows, in case you prefer to download via the command line.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. All video processing happens locally in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP — no passwords stored. The extension does not collect or transmit any personal data.
