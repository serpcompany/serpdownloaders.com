#### FAQ

**Q: What GoHighLevel pages does this work with?**
**A:** Any GoHighLevel-powered portal, membership area, or course page. The extension detects the GoHighLevel environment automatically using DOM markers like `x-cp-build-version` and `clientportal-siteCustomHeader`, as well as GoHighLevel/HighLevel hostname patterns.

**Q: What video platforms are supported?**
**A:** GoHighLevel native HLS video, Loom, Vimeo, Wistia, and YouTube embeds. Each platform has a dedicated detector and handler for reliable extraction.

**Q: How do I download a video?**
**A:** Go to any lesson or page in your GoHighLevel portal and press play on the video. Then click the extension icon in your toolbar.

**Q: What quality options are available?**
**A:** The extension detects all available qualities from the embedded platform or the GoHighLevel HLS manifest. Options depend on what the video host provides -- typically multiple resolutions up to 1080p or higher.

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All videos are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player. GoHighLevel native HLS streams are automatically converted to MP4 during download.

**Q: Can I download multiple videos at once?**
**A:** Yes -- the extension supports up to 3 concurrent downloads with individual progress tracking.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited downloads.

**Q: Does this work on Skool, Circle, or Kajabi?**
**A:** No -- Skool, Circle, and Kajabi each have their own dedicated SERP downloader extensions. This extension is specifically for GoHighLevel portals.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes -- supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Why isn't the extension finding my video?**
**A:** Make sure you are on a GoHighLevel portal page. Press play on the video first. The extension needs the stream to start before detection works. If issues persist, refresh the page.

**Q: Does this handle GoHighLevel native video (not just embeds)?**
**A:** Yes. GoHighLevel uses HLS streaming for its native video player (Plyr). The extension discovers the m3u8 manifest, fetches all segments, and stitches them into a downloadable MP4 file.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. Video processing happens entirely in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP -- no passwords stored.
