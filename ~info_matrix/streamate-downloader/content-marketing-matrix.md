#### FAQ

**Q: How do I capture a live stream?**
**A:** Go to any live model's page on Streamate.com. Click the download button or the extension icon. Select your quality and click Start. The extension captures the stream in real time. Click Stop when you want to finish — the recording saves as MP4.

**Q: Can I download recorded videos too?**
**A:** Yes. Navigate to a model's video page (VOD) and use the extension just like any video downloader — select quality and download.

**Q: What quality options are available?**
**A:** The extension detects all available stream qualities from the source — typically multiple resolutions. Formats are sorted by quality (highest first).

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All videos are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player.

**Q: Where are my downloads saved?**
**A:** Videos automatically save to a `STREAMATE` subfolder inside your browser's default Downloads directory.

**Q: Does this support VR streams?**
**A:** Yes. The extension detects VR camera settings and includes VR metadata (stereoPacking, frameFormat, angle) in the filename.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited downloads.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Why can't the extension find the stream?**
**A:** Make sure the model is currently live. If they are offline or in a private show, the extension will show a status message. Refresh the page if issues persist.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. Stream processing happens entirely in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP — no passwords stored.
