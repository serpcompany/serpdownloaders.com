#### FAQ

**Q: How do I download a video?**
**A:** Go to any page with a Wistia video and click the extension icon. The extension auto-detects the embedded player. Select your quality and click Download.

**Q: Does it work on sites other than wistia.com?**
**A:** Yes — it detects Wistia players embedded on any website. Course platforms, marketing pages, knowledge bases — anywhere Wistia is used.

**Q: What quality options are available?**
**A:** The extension fetches all available renditions from the Wistia API — typically multiple resolutions including the original upload quality. Formats are sorted by quality (highest first).

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All videos are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player. HLS streams are automatically converted.

**Q: Where are my downloads saved?**
**A:** The extension uses your browser's Save As dialog, so you choose where to save each download.

**Q: Can I download playlists or channels?**
**A:** Yes — the extension detects Wistia playlists and channels, shows all videos in a browsable list, and lets you download each one individually.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited downloads.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: What about password-protected videos?**
**A:** The extension detects password-protected Wistia videos and notifies you. Password-protected videos cannot be downloaded.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. Video processing happens entirely in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP — no passwords stored.
