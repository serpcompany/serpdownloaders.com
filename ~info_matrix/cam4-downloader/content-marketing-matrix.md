#### FAQ

**Q: How do I capture a live stream?**
**A:** Go to any model's page on Cam4.com while they are live. Click the Start button on the player or in the extension popup. The extension begins recording HLS segments in real time. Click Stop when you want to end the capture — the stream saves as MP4.

**Q: Can I download recorded videos too?**
**A:** Yes. Navigate to a model's video page (/{model}/videos/{id}) and the extension detects the VOD content. Select quality and download — it saves as MP4.

**Q: What quality options are available?**
**A:** The extension parses the HLS master manifest and shows all available resolution variants — typically ranging from 360p to 1080p, some with frame rate labels (e.g. 720p @ 60fps). Quality is sorted highest first.

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All captures and downloads are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player.

**Q: Where are my downloads saved?**
**A:** Videos automatically save to a `CAM4` subfolder inside your browser's default Downloads directory.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited captures and downloads.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Why does the extension say the model is offline?**
**A:** The model must be actively streaming for live capture to work. If they are offline or in a private show, the extension will display a status message. Wait for the public stream to resume.

**Q: What is Mouflon DRM?**
**A:** Mouflon is an HLS manifest obfuscation system that encrypts segment filenames. The extension handles this automatically — no action required from you.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. All stream processing and capture happens entirely in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP — no passwords stored.
