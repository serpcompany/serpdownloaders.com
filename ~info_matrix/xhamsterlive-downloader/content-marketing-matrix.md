#### FAQ

**Q: How do I capture a live stream?**
**A:** Go to a model's page on XHamsterLive.com while they're live. Click the extension icon or the player download button, select quality, and click Start. Click Stop when you want to end the recording. The captured stream saves as MP4.

**Q: How do I download a saved recording (VOD)?**
**A:** Navigate to a model's video recording page (the URL contains /videos/). Open the extension, select quality, and click Download. The recording saves as MP4.

**Q: Does this work with VR streams?**
**A:** Yes. The extension detects VR streams automatically and includes VR metadata (stereoPacking, frameFormat, angle) in the filename. A VR badge appears in the popup for VR content.

**Q: What quality options are available?**
**A:** The extension detects all available quality levels from the HLS master playlist. For live streams, qualities vary by the model's broadcast settings. Formats are sorted by resolution (highest first).

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All videos — live captures, VOD recordings, and VR streams — are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player.

**Q: Where are my downloads saved?**
**A:** Videos automatically save to an `XHamsterLive` subfolder inside your browser's default Downloads directory.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited downloads.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: What if the model is offline or in a private show?**
**A:** The extension will show a status message. Live capture only works when the model is streaming publicly. Saved VOD recordings can be downloaded anytime.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. All stream processing and HLS-to-MP4 conversion happens entirely in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP — no passwords stored.
