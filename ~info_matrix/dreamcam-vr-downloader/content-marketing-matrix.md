#### FAQ

**Q: How do I record a live stream?**
**A:** Go to any model page on DreamCamVR while the stream is live. Open the extension popup or click the download button on the player. Select your quality and click Start. The extension captures the stream in real-time. Click Stop when you want to finish — the captured stream converts to MP4.

**Q: Can I download saved recordings (VOD)?**
**A:** Yes. Navigate to a model's video page (e.g. /username/videos/12345) and the extension will detect the recording and offer it for download.

**Q: Does it support VR streams?**
**A:** Yes. The extension detects VR camera settings (stereo packing, frame format, horizontal angle) and includes them in the VR badge and filename suffix.

**Q: What quality options are available?**
**A:** The extension parses the HLS master manifest and shows all available resolutions with frame rate info. Formats are sorted by quality (highest first).

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All captures are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited captures.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Why does it say the model is offline?**
**A:** The model needs to be actively streaming for live capture. If the model is offline or in a private show, wait for the public stream to resume.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. Stream processing happens entirely in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP — no passwords stored.
