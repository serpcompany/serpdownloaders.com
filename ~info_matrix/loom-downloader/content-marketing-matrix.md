#### FAQ

**Q: How do I download a Loom video?**
**A:** Navigate to any Loom share page (loom.com/share/...) or any webpage with a Loom embed. Click the download button that appears on the video player, or click the extension icon in your toolbar.

**Q: Can I download Loom videos embedded on other websites?**
**A:** Yes. The extension detects Loom embeds on any website — Notion pages, Confluence, project management tools, blog posts, and more. A download overlay button appears on each detected embed.

**Q: What about password-protected Loom videos?**
**A:** The extension supports password-protected videos. When you try to download one, you will be prompted to enter the password. Once entered, the download proceeds normally.

**Q: What quality options are available?**
**A:** Quality depends on how the Loom recording was captured. The extension detects all available quality variants from the HLS or DASH manifest and lets you choose.

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All videos are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player.

**Q: Can I download multiple Loom videos at once?**
**A:** The extension supports a download queue with up to 3 concurrent downloads. When multiple Loom embeds are detected on a page, you can select which ones to download.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** Sign in with your email to start your free trial. Purchase a license for unlimited downloads.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Why is my download taking time?**
**A:** Loom videos use HLS or DASH streaming. The extension downloads all stream segments and merges them into an MP4 file in your browser. Longer videos and higher quality take more time. Progress is displayed in real time.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. All video processing happens entirely in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP email verification — no passwords stored. The extension communicates only with Loom's public API and the SERP auth service.
