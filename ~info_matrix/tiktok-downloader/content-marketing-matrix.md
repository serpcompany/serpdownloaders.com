#### FAQ

**Q: How do I download a video?**
**A:** Browse TikTok normally. Download overlay buttons appear on every video in your feed. Click the download button on any video. You can also visit a single video page and use the extension popup.

**Q: What quality options are available?**
**A:** The extension detects all available qualities from TikTok's bitrateInfo data — typically multiple resolutions. The best available quality is used automatically.

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All videos are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player.

**Q: Do downloads have watermarks?**
**A:** No. Unlike third-party download websites, this extension downloads directly from TikTok's CDN, so there are no added watermarks.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited downloads.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Why isn't the extension finding my video?**
**A:** Try scrolling past the video and back, or refreshing the page. TikTok loads video data dynamically, and the extension needs the feed data to load before detection works.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. Video processing happens entirely in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP — no passwords stored. The extension only accesses TikTok pages.

**Q: Can I turn off the download overlays?**
**A:** Yes. Open the extension popup and toggle "Show Download Overlays" off. You can still download via the popup.
