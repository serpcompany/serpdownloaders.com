#### FAQ

**Q: How do I record a live stream?**
**A:** Go to any model page on Stripchat VR while the stream is live. Click the Start button in the popup or the download button on the player. Select quality and begin capture. Click Stop when you're done.

**Q: Does this support VR content?**
**A:** Yes. The extension detects VR camera settings (stereoPacking, frameFormat, horizontal angle) and includes them in the filename. A VR badge shows the configuration details in the popup.

**Q: What quality options are available?**
**A:** The extension detects all available HLS quality levels from the stream — typically multiple resolutions. Formats are sorted by quality (highest first).

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All videos are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device, media player, or VR headset.

**Q: Can I download recorded/VOD content too?**
**A:** Yes. The extension supports both live stream capture and VOD downloads from Stripchat video pages.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited captures.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: What if the model is offline or in a private show?**
**A:** The extension will show a status message indicating the model is offline or in a private show. You need to wait for the public stream to resume.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. Stream processing happens entirely in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP — no passwords stored.
