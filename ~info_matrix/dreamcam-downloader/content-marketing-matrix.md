#### FAQ

**Q: How do I record a live stream?**
**A:** Go to any live model page on DreamCam. Click the extension icon or the download button on the player. Click "Start" to begin recording. Click "Stop" when you want to finish — the recording saves as MP4.

**Q: Can I download recorded videos (VODs)?**
**A:** Yes. Navigate to a model's video page (e.g., `/username/videos/123`). The extension detects it as a recording and offers quality options for direct download.

**Q: What quality options are available?**
**A:** The extension parses all available HLS variants from the master manifest — typically multiple resolutions. Formats are sorted by quality (highest first).

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All videos are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player.

**Q: Where are my downloads saved?**
**A:** Videos automatically save to a `DREAMCAM` subfolder inside your browser's default Downloads directory.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited captures and downloads.

**Q: Does this support VR streams?**
**A:** Yes. The extension detects VR camera settings (stereo packing, frame format, horizontal angle) and includes VR metadata in the filename for proper playback in VR players.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Why isn't the extension finding the stream?**
**A:** Make sure the model is currently live and streaming. If they're offline or in a private show, the extension will show a status message. Refresh the page and try again.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. All stream processing happens entirely in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP — no passwords stored.
