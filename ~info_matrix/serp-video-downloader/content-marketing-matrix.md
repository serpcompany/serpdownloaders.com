#### FAQ

**Q: What sites does this work on?**
**A:** It works on virtually any website. Dedicated support for YouTube, Vimeo, Loom, Wistia, SproutVideo, and Skool, plus generic detection for any site with HTML5 video, HLS, or DASH streams.

**Q: What quality options are available?**
**A:** The extension detects all available qualities from the source. Formats are sorted by quality (highest first), with combined audio+video streams preferred. MP4 format is prioritized.

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All videos are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player. HLS and DASH streams are automatically converted to MP4.

**Q: What if there are multiple videos on a page?**
**A:** The extension detects all videos and shows a dropdown selector so you can choose which one to download.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited downloads.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Can I use my license from another SERP downloader?**
**A:** Yes. Licenses from the SERP Downloaders Bundle, SERP Video Downloaders Bundle, or any individual SERP downloader (Vimeo, YouTube, Loom, Wistia, Skool, M3U8, and more) are accepted.

**Q: Why isn't the extension finding my video?**
**A:** Make sure the video has started playing or loading on the page. Some sites require the video to begin before streams are available. Refresh the page and try again if needed.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. All video processing happens entirely in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP email verification — no passwords stored.
