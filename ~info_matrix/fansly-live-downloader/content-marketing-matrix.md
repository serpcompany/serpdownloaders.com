#### FAQ

**Q: How do I record a live stream?**
**A:** Go to any live model page on Fansly.com. Open the extension popup or click the download button on the player. Select your quality and click Start. The extension records the stream in real time. Click Stop when you want to end the recording.

**Q: Can I download VOD recordings too?**
**A:** Yes. Navigate to a model's video page (e.g., /username/videos/12345) and the extension works as a standard downloader -- select quality and click Download.

**Q: What quality options are available?**
**A:** The extension detects all available HLS variants from the stream -- typically multiple resolutions. Formats are listed by resolution (e.g., 1080p, 720p) with bandwidth information.

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All videos are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player.

**Q: Where are my downloads saved?**
**A:** Videos automatically save to a `FANSLYLIVE` subfolder inside your browser's default Downloads directory.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited use.

**Q: Does this support VR streams?**
**A:** Yes. The extension detects VR streams and includes VR camera settings (stereo packing, frame format, horizontal angle) in the download metadata and filename.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes -- supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Why isn't the extension finding the stream?**
**A:** Make sure the model is actually live and streaming. If the model is offline or in a private show, the extension will display a status message. Refresh the page if issues persist.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. Stream processing happens entirely in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP -- no passwords stored.
