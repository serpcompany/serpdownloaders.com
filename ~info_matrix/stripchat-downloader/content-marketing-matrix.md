#### FAQ

**Q: How do I record a live stream?**
**A:** Go to any model's page on Stripchat.com while they are live. Click the download button on the player or the extension icon. Select your preferred quality and click Download. The extension starts recording and shows elapsed time. Click Stop when you want to end the recording.

**Q: Can I download recorded videos too?**
**A:** Yes. Navigate to any video on a model's profile page (the /videos/ section). The extension detects the video source and lets you download it as MP4.

**Q: What quality options are available?**
**A:** The extension detects all available qualities from Stripchat's HLS manifest — typically multiple resolutions up to 1080p. Formats include frame rate information when available.

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All videos and recordings are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited downloads.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Why does the extension say the model is offline?**
**A:** The model must be actively streaming for live capture to work. If the model is offline or in a private show, the extension will display a status message. Wait for the public stream to resume.

**Q: What if the model is in a private show?**
**A:** Private shows cannot be recorded. The extension will display a message asking you to wait until the public stream resumes.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. Stream recording and video processing happen entirely in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP — no passwords stored.
