#### FAQ

**Q: How do I capture a live stream?**
**A:** Go to any model's page on MyFreeCams.com while the stream is live. Click the download button on the player or the extension icon. Select your quality and click Start. The extension captures the stream in real time. Click Stop when you're done — it saves as MP4.

**Q: Can I download recorded videos?**
**A:** Yes. Navigate to a model's video page and use the extension to download the recording directly.

**Q: What quality options are available?**
**A:** The extension detects all available qualities from the HLS master manifest — typically multiple resolutions up to the source quality. Formats are sorted by quality (highest first).

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All videos are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player.

**Q: Where are my downloads saved?**
**A:** Videos automatically save to a `MYFREECAMS` subfolder inside your browser's default Downloads directory.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited downloads.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Does it support VR streams?**
**A:** Yes. The extension detects VR metadata (stereoPacking, frameFormat, horizontalAngle) and preserves it in the filename.

**Q: Why isn't the extension finding the stream?**
**A:** Make sure the model is online and the stream is live. If the model is offline or in a private show, the extension will display a status message. Refresh the page and try again.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. Video processing happens entirely in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP — no passwords stored.
