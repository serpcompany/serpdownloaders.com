#### FAQ

**Q: How do I download a LinkedIn video?**
**A:** Go to a LinkedIn page with video content (feed, post, or video page), then click the extension icon. It scans the page and shows detected videos. Click download on any video.

**Q: What types of content can I download?**
**A:** Videos, images, and post text. Videos save as MP4, images save in their original format (JPG, PNG, etc.), and text posts save as TXT files.

**Q: Does this capture every image on the page?**
**A:** No — it uses smart filtering. Profile photos, company logos, icons, emojis, and small UI elements are excluded. Only meaningful post and article images are captured.

**Q: Where are my downloads saved?**
**A:** Files save to a `LinkedIn Downloader` folder inside your Downloads directory, organized by author name. Filenames include the date when available.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited downloads.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Can I download from any LinkedIn page?**
**A:** The extension works on feed pages, individual post pages (`/posts/`), video pages (`/video/`), activity update pages (`/feed/update/`), and recent activity pages.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. All content scanning and processing happens locally in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP — no passwords stored. The extension only reads content you can already see on the page.
