#### FAQ

**Q: How do I download a Tella video?**
**A:** Go to any Tella.tv video page and press play. Then click the download button that appears on the player, or click the extension icon in your toolbar.

**Q: Does this work with Tella videos embedded on other websites?**
**A:** Yes. The extension scans all frames on the page and detects Tella embeds on any website, not just tella.tv.

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All videos are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player.

**Q: Why do I need to press play first?**
**A:** Tella uses signed streaming URLs that are only generated when playback starts. The extension needs the stream to load before it can detect and download the video.

**Q: What if the download fails with an expired URL error?**
**A:** The extension automatically attempts to refresh expired manifest URLs. If it still fails, press play on the video again and retry the download.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited downloads.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. All video processing happens entirely in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP — no passwords stored.

**Q: Can I download my own Tella recordings?**
**A:** Yes. If you can view the recording in your browser, the extension can detect and download it.
