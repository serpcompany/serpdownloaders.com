#### FAQ

**Q: How do I download a video?**
**A:** Go to any OnlyFans page with video content. Hover over the video and click the download overlay button, or open the extension's side panel and select the video from the list.

**Q: Can I download images and GIFs too?**
**A:** Yes. The extension detects videos, images, GIFs, and text posts. Each media item gets a download overlay button. The side panel organizes content by type.

**Q: How does bulk download work?**
**A:** Open the Bulk tab in the side panel. The extension auto-scrolls through the creator's media page, detects all content, and downloads everything. You can filter by media type (all, photos, or videos).

**Q: What quality options are available?**
**A:** The extension detects all available qualities from the source. For videos, common options include 720p, 1080p, source, and original. Quality is parsed from the video URL.

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All videos are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player. HLS streams are automatically converted.

**Q: Where are my downloads saved?**
**A:** Videos save to an `OnlyFans/Videos/` or `OnlyFans/{creator}/Videos/` subfolder inside your Downloads directory. For bulk operations, you can choose a custom folder.

**Q: Why does a video show "DRM Protected"?**
**A:** Some OnlyFans content uses DRM encryption. The extension detects this and notifies you. DRM-protected videos cannot be downloaded at this time.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. Video processing happens entirely in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP — no passwords stored. The extension accesses OnlyFans using your existing logged-in session cookies.
