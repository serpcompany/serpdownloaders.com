#### FAQ

**Q: How do I download a video from Kajabi?**
**A:** Open a Kajabi lesson page with a video and press play. Then click the extension icon in your toolbar. The extension detects the video platform and shows available quality options. Select your quality and click Download.

**Q: Which video platforms are supported?**
**A:** The extension detects videos hosted on Loom, Vimeo, YouTube, and Wistia. These are the most common video hosting platforms used in Kajabi courses.

**Q: What quality options are available?**
**A:** Quality depends on the hosting platform and how the course creator uploaded the video. The extension shows all available quality levels for each video.

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All videos are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player.

**Q: Can I download multiple videos at once?**
**A:** Yes — the extension supports up to 3 concurrent downloads with an in-page download manager showing progress for each.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited downloads.

**Q: Does this work on custom Kajabi domains?**
**A:** Yes — the extension works on *.kajabi.com, *.mykajabi.com, and custom domains that use Kajabi's platform.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Why isn't the extension finding my video?**
**A:** Press play on the video first. The extension needs the video player to load before it can detect the hosting platform. If issues persist, refresh the page and try again.

**Q: Is this legal?**
**A:** This extension is intended for downloading content you own or have purchased access to. Users are responsible for ensuring they have the right to download content.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. Video processing happens entirely in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP — no passwords stored.
