#### FAQ

**Q: How do I download a SproutVideo video?**
**A:** Navigate to any page with a SproutVideo embed. The extension detects it automatically. Click the overlay button on the player or click the extension icon, select your quality, and download.

**Q: Does this work with videos embedded on other websites?**
**A:** Yes. The extension detects SproutVideo embeds on any website, not just sproutvideo.com. If a page has an embedded SproutVideo player or iframe, it will be detected.

**Q: Can I download password-protected videos?**
**A:** Yes. If a video is password-protected, the extension will prompt you to enter the password. Once unlocked, you can download normally.

**Q: What quality options are available?**
**A:** The extension provides all available formats from the SproutVideo player data, including direct download options (source, UHD, HD, SD) and authenticated HLS streams. Direct downloads are preferred over HLS.

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All videos are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after activation. Purchase a license for unlimited downloads.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Why isn't the extension finding the video?**
**A:** Make sure the SproutVideo embed has loaded on the page. If the video is inside an iframe, the extension scans iframes automatically. Try refreshing the page if the embed loaded after the extension.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. All video processing happens entirely in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP verification through auth.serp.co. No passwords or video data are sent to external servers.

**Q: What is SproutVideo?**
**A:** SproutVideo is a professional video hosting platform used by businesses for marketing videos, training content, course materials, and internal communications. Videos are typically embedded on company websites and portals.
