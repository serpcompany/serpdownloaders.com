#### FAQ

**Q: How do I download a video from Pinterest?**
**A:** Go to any Pinterest page and click the extension icon. The popup shows all detected videos in the Videos tab. Click download on the one you want, or use "Download Visible" to save all of them.

**Q: Can I download images too?**
**A:** Yes. The extension detects videos, images, and text posts. Switch to the Images tab in the popup to see and download all detected images.

**Q: What about text posts?**
**A:** Text from visible pins is extracted and available in the Text tab. Text posts are saved as `.txt` files with the post URL and timestamp included.

**Q: What quality are downloaded videos?**
**A:** The extension automatically selects the best available quality. It checks multiple sources — PinResource API, inline data, DOM elements — and picks the highest-resolution progressive MP4 available.

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All videos are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player. HLS streams are automatically converted to MP4 in your browser.

**Q: Where are my downloads saved?**
**A:** Files save to a `Pinterest Downloader` subfolder inside your browser's default Downloads directory, organized by source.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited downloads.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Can I download from boards and profiles?**
**A:** Yes. The extension works on any Pinterest page — home feed, boards, profiles, search results, and individual pin pages.

**Q: Why isn't the extension finding any media?**
**A:** Make sure you are on a Pinterest.com page. Pins must be visible (loaded) on the page. Try scrolling to load more content, then click the Rescan button in the popup.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. All processing happens entirely in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP — no passwords stored.
