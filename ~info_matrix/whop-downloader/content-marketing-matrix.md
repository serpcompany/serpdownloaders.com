#### FAQ

**Q: How do I download a video from Whop?**
**A:** Go to any page on Whop.com that contains video content. Click the extension icon, switch to the Videos tab, and click Download on any video. You can also use the overlay button that appears directly on the video player.

**Q: Can I download images and text too?**
**A:** Yes. The extension detects videos, images, and text posts. Use the tabs in the popup to switch between content types and download what you need.

**Q: What about Mux-hosted videos?**
**A:** The extension automatically detects Mux players and handles HLS stream conversion. Videos are saved as standard MP4 files.

**Q: Can I download everything at once?**
**A:** Yes. Click "Download Visible" to start downloading all detected items in the active tab (Videos, Images, or Text).

**Q: Where are my downloads saved?**
**A:** Files save to your browser's default Downloads directory.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited downloads.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Why isn't the extension finding my content?**
**A:** Make sure you're on a Whop.com page with visible content. Click "Rescan" in the popup to re-detect. For videos, press play first so the stream URL becomes available.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. All processing happens entirely in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP — no passwords stored.
