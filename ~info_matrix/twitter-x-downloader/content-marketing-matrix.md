#### FAQ

**Q: How do I download a video from X (Twitter)?**
**A:** Go to any X page, click the extension icon, and switch to the Videos tab. Click Download on the video you want, or use "Download Visible" to get them all.

**Q: Can I download images too?**
**A:** Yes. The extension detects images from X posts. Switch to the Images tab in the popup to see and download them.

**Q: What about post text?**
**A:** Yes. Switch to the Text tab to see extracted post content. Download saves it as a TXT file.

**Q: What quality options are available?**
**A:** The extension detects all available quality variants from X's video API. It scores and ranks by bitrate, preferring MP4 over HLS manifests, and downloads the best available quality.

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All videos are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player.

**Q: Where are my downloads saved?**
**A:** Media saves to a `Twitter X Downloader` subfolder inside your browser's default Downloads directory, organized by source and title.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited downloads.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Why isn't the extension finding my video?**
**A:** Scroll the post into view so the video loads. Then click Rescan. The extension needs the media to be present in the page DOM or network activity.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. All processing happens locally in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP — no passwords stored.
