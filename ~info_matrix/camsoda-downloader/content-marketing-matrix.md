#### FAQ

**Q: How do I record a live stream?**
**A:** Open a model page on CamSoda.com while they're live. Click the download button or extension icon, select your quality, and click Start. The capture runs until you click Stop or the stream ends. The recording saves as MP4.

**Q: Can I download recorded videos too?**
**A:** Yes. Navigate to a model's video page (VOD) and the extension will detect and download the recording. Some videos may require purchase or fan-club access on CamSoda.

**Q: What quality options are available?**
**A:** The extension parses the HLS master manifest and shows all available quality variants — typically multiple resolutions sorted by quality (highest first).

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All captures and downloads are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player.

**Q: Where are my downloads saved?**
**A:** Videos save to your browser's default Downloads directory.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited capture.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Why isn't the extension detecting the stream?**
**A:** Make sure the model is live and the stream is playing. The extension needs the stream to be active before it can detect it. If the model is offline or in a private show, you'll see a status message.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. Stream capture and processing happen entirely in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP — no passwords stored.
