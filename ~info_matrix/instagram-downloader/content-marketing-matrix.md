#### FAQ

**Q: How do I download an Instagram Reel?**
**A:** Navigate to any Reel on Instagram. The extension adds a "Download video" button directly on the Reel. Click it and the video saves as MP4. You can also open the extension popup for more options.

**Q: Can I download all images from a carousel post?**
**A:** Yes. Carousel posts show a "Download images (N)" button where N is the number of images. Click to download all of them at once. You can also download individual images using the per-image download icon.

**Q: What about Instagram Stories?**
**A:** The extension detects Stories from their URL pattern (`/stories/{username}/{id}`) and allows downloading videos and images from visible Stories.

**Q: What formats are downloaded files saved in?**
**A:** Videos are saved as MP4 files. Images keep their original format (JPEG, PNG, WebP, etc.). Post text is saved as a plain text file with the post URL and timestamp.

**Q: Where are my downloads saved?**
**A:** Media saves to an `Instagram Downloader` subfolder inside your browser's default Downloads directory, organized by source and title.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** Sign in with your email to get free trial downloads. Purchase a license for unlimited downloads.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Why is the extension not detecting a video?**
**A:** Make sure the post or Reel is visible and loaded on the page. Try scrolling to it or refreshing. For feed posts, the video may need to start playing. Use the "Rescan" button in the popup if needed.

**Q: Can I download from private accounts?**
**A:** The extension works with content visible in your browser. If you are logged in and can see a private account's posts, the extension can detect and download them.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. All media processing happens locally in your browser. The extension reads Instagram's public CDN URLs and your own session data. Authentication uses secure OTP — no passwords stored. No data is sent to third-party servers.
