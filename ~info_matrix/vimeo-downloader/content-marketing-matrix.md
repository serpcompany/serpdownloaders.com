#### FAQ

**Q: How do I download a video?**
**A:** Go to any Vimeo video page or a page with an embedded Vimeo player. Click the download button on the player or the extension icon in your toolbar. Select your quality and click Download.

**Q: Does this work with embedded Vimeo videos on other websites?**
**A:** Yes. The extension detects Vimeo player iframes on any website -- course platforms, portfolios, event pages, and more.

**Q: What quality options are available?**
**A:** The extension detects all available qualities from Vimeo's stream data -- typically multiple resolutions with different bitrates. Progressive (direct MP4) and HLS streams are both supported.

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All videos are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player.

**Q: Can I download password-protected videos?**
**A:** Yes. If a video is password-protected, the popup will prompt you to enter the password before downloading.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited downloads.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes -- supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Why isn't the extension finding my video?**
**A:** Make sure the Vimeo player has loaded on the page. If the page has multiple Vimeo embeds, the extension will show a selection list. Try refreshing the page if detection fails.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. Video processing happens entirely in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP -- no passwords stored.
