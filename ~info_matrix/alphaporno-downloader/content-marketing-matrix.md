#### FAQ

**Q: How do I download a video?**
**A:** Go to any video page on AlphaPorno.com and press play. Then either click the download button that appears on the player, or click the extension icon in your toolbar. Select your preferred quality and hit download.

**Q: What quality options are available?**
**A:** The extension offers whatever qualities the source video provides — typically 360p, 480p, 720p, and sometimes 1080p. It auto-detects available resolutions.

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All videos are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player.

**Q: Where are my downloads saved?**
**A:** Videos are automatically saved to a `AlphaPorno` subfolder inside your browser's default Downloads directory.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** You get 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. After that, purchase a license for unlimited downloads.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Why isn't the extension finding my video?**
**A:** Make sure you've pressed play on the video first. The extension needs the stream to start before it can detect it. If issues persist, try refreshing the page.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. Video processing happens entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded. Authentication uses secure OTP (one-time password) — no passwords stored.
