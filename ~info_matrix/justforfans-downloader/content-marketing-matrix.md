#### FAQ

**Q: How do I download content from JustForFans?**
**A:** Go to any creator's page on JustFor.Fans. Click the extension icon to see detected content organized by type (Videos, Images, Text). Click "Download Visible" to save everything, or use the download button on individual posts.

**Q: What types of content can I download?**
**A:** Videos (saved as MP4), images (full resolution, including gallery/carousel posts), and text posts.

**Q: What quality options are available?**
**A:** The extension detects all available video qualities from the source. Formats are sorted by quality (highest first), with MP4 preferred over HLS.

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All videos are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player. HLS and DASH streams are converted automatically.

**Q: Where are my downloads saved?**
**A:** Content automatically saves to a `JustForFans` subfolder inside your browser's default Downloads directory.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited downloads.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Why isn't the extension finding my content?**
**A:** Scroll through the creator's feed to load content first. The extension scans visible posts on the page. If issues persist, click the "Rescan" button or refresh the page.

**Q: Can I download gallery/carousel posts?**
**A:** Yes. The extension automatically expands gallery and carousel posts to capture all images in multi-image posts.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. All processing happens entirely in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP — no passwords stored. The extension only accesses JustFor.Fans pages.

**Q: What if a video uses DRM/encryption?**
**A:** The extension includes DASH decryption support for encrypted streams commonly used on JustForFans. Most protected content can be downloaded.
