#### FAQ

**Q: Which 123Movies sites does this work on?**
**A:** It works on all 123Movies mirrors and variants — any domain containing "123movies" or "123-movies" is automatically detected.

**Q: What format are the downloaded videos?**
**A:** All videos are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player.

**Q: Do I need to press play first?**
**A:** Yes — the extension detects the video stream when you start playback. Press play, then click the extension icon.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** You get 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. After that, you can upgrade for unlimited downloads.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes — SERP 123Movies Downloader supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Why isn't the extension detecting my video?**
**A:** Make sure you've pressed play on the video first. Some sites require playback to start before the stream can be captured. If the issue persists, try refreshing the page.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** The extension only processes video data locally in your browser. Authentication uses a secure OTP (one-time password) system — no passwords are stored.
