#### FAQ

**Q: How do I record a live stream?**
**A:** Open a model's room on Flirt4Free.com while they're live. Click the extension icon or in-page button, select quality, and click Start. Click Stop when you want to end the recording.

**Q: Can I download recorded videos too?**
**A:** Yes. Navigate to a model's video gallery page and the extension will detect available recordings. Select quality and click Download.

**Q: What quality options are available?**
**A:** The extension parses the HLS master manifest and shows all available quality variants -- typically multiple resolutions. For VOD, source MP4 or HLS variants are offered.

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All videos are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player.

**Q: Where are my downloads saved?**
**A:** Videos automatically save to a `FLIRT4FREE` subfolder inside your browser's default Downloads directory.

**Q: Does this support VR streams?**
**A:** Yes. The extension detects VR camera settings and includes VR metadata in the filename (stereoPacking, frame format, angle).

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited downloads.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes -- supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Why isn't the extension finding the stream?**
**A:** The model must be live and in a public show. Private shows and offline models are not accessible. If issues persist, refresh the page.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. All stream processing happens entirely in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP -- no passwords stored.
