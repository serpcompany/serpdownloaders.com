#### FAQ

**Q: What portals does this work with?**
**A:** Any GoHighLevel or ClientClub-powered client portal. The extension detects the portal environment automatically using DOM markers like `x-cp-build-version` and `clientportal-siteCustomHeader`.

**Q: What video platforms are supported?**
**A:** Loom, Vimeo, Wistia, YouTube embeds, and GoHighLevel native video. Each platform has a dedicated detector and handler for reliable extraction.

**Q: How do I download a video?**
**A:** Go to any lesson or page in your ClientClub portal and press play on the video. Then click the extension icon or right-click and select "Download ClientClub Video."

**Q: What quality options are available?**
**A:** The extension detects all available qualities from the embedded platform. Options depend on what the video host provides -- typically multiple resolutions up to 1080p or higher.

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** All videos are saved as standard MP4 files that play on any device or media player.

**Q: Where are my downloads saved?**
**A:** Videos automatically save to a `ClientClub Downloader` subfolder inside your browser's default Downloads directory.

**Q: Can I download multiple videos at once?**
**A:** Yes -- the extension supports up to 3 concurrent downloads with individual progress tracking.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited downloads.

**Q: Does this work on Skool or Circle?**
**A:** No -- Skool and Circle have their own dedicated SERP downloader extensions. This extension is specifically for GoHighLevel/ClientClub portals.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** Yes -- supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale, Yandex, and Firefox.

**Q: Why isn't the extension finding my video?**
**A:** Make sure you are on a GoHighLevel/ClientClub portal page. Press play on the video first. The extension needs the stream to start before detection works. If issues persist, refresh the page.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. Video processing happens entirely in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP -- no passwords stored.
