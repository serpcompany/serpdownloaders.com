#### FAQ

**Q: How do I download a video from my GoKollab course?**
**A:** Go to the course page, click the extension icon, and the popup will show detected videos. Click any video to download, or use "Download Visible" to get everything on the page.

**Q: What video platforms are supported?**
**A:** The extension detects videos from Loom, YouTube, Vimeo, Wistia, GoHighLevel's native player, and Circle. It automatically identifies which platform is used.

**Q: Does this work on any website?**
**A:** No. The extension uses intelligent platform gating and only activates on GoKollab and GoHighLevel-powered client portals. It checks for specific HighLevel platform markers before running.

**Q: Can I download images and text too?**
**A:** Yes. The popup has three tabs — Videos, Images/GIFs, and Text. You can download assets from any tab individually or use the bulk download button.

**Q: What format are downloaded videos?**
**A:** Videos are typically saved as MP4 files. The exact format depends on the source platform and selected quality.

**Q: How many free downloads do I get?**
**A:** 3 free trial downloads after signing in with your email. Purchase a license for unlimited downloads.

**Q: Does this work on Firefox?**
**A:** The extension is built for Chromium-based browsers — Chrome, Edge, Brave, and Opera. Firefox support may vary.

**Q: Why doesn't the extension detect anything on my page?**
**A:** The extension only activates on GoKollab/GoHighLevel pages. If you're on a different platform, it won't scan. If you are on the right platform, try pressing play on any video first, then click Rescan.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes. All video processing happens locally in your browser. Authentication uses secure OTP — no passwords stored.
