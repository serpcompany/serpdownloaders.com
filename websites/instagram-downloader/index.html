Instagram Downloader by SERP

serp.ly/instagram-downloader

Download Instagram videos, Reels, photos & Stories. In-page buttons on every post. Saves as MP4 or original format. Free trial.

Overview

SERP Instagram Downloader is a browser extension that saves videos, photos, Reels, Stories, and post text from Instagram to your computer. Browse Instagram normally, and the extension adds download buttons directly on posts. Click to save any media as MP4 or its original image format. No external tools, no copy-pasting links. Free trial included.

Why It Exists

Purpose-built for Instagram with multi-source video detection (DOM, GraphQL API, CDN monitoring, inline data), in-page download buttons on every post, carousel image support, Reels and Stories download, and automatic text export. Everything happens in your browser — no external tools required.

Offline use cases

  • Save Instagram Reels for offline viewing
  • Download photos from carousel posts
  • Archive your own Instagram content
  • Save post text and captions
  • Download Stories before they expire
  • Bulk-download all visible media on a profile or feed page
  • Save IGTV videos
